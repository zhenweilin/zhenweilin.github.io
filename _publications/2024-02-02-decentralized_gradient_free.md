We develop DGFM and DGFM+ for decentralized optimization of nonsmooth, nonconvex objectives using function evaluations. The methods combine randomized smoothing and gradient tracking, with variance reduction in DGFM+ improving the oracle complexity for reaching approximate Goldstein stationarity.

Published in *Proceedings of the AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence* **38** (16), 17477–17486 (2024).
